The best time for a photoshoot in Alanya is the last hour before sunset, when the light is soft and the heat drops. In early July that means roughly 19:15 to 20:15; in December, about 16:40 to 17:40. Choose the place for the look you want: Cleopatra Beach, or the castle and harbour. To book, send your date on WhatsApp.

Below is everything worth deciding before the day: time, place, clothes and how booking works.

## When to shoot: time by season

Plan the shoot around sunset, not around the clock. Turkey stays on one time zone all year (UTC+3) with no daylight saving change, so the gap between summer and winter is large.

| Month | Sunset in Alanya (approx.) | Start the shoot around |
|---|---|---|
| June and July | 20:04 to 20:14 | 19:05 to 19:15 |
| August | 19:59 to 19:45 | 18:45 to 19:00 |
| September | 19:22 to 19:01 | 18:00 to 18:20 |
| October | 18:37 to 18:17 | 17:15 to 17:40 |
| November to February | 17:37 to 18:32 | 16:40 to 17:30 |
| March to May | 18:46 to 19:51 | 17:45 to 18:50 |

Half-monthly times are in our post on [sunset times in Alanya](/en/blog/sunset-times-alanya/).

Early morning is the other good window. The beach is quiet and the sun is still gentle. The worst slot is a summer midday: hard light, dark shadows under the eyes and serious heat in July and August.

## Where to shoot

Four kinds of places work well. They are public places, so check rules and opening hours locally.

- **Beach and sea.** Cleopatra Beach sits on the west side of the peninsula and faces the sunset. Sand, clear water and the sun dropping into the sea.
- **Castle and harbour.** Alanya Castle walls, the Red Tower (Kızıl Kule) and the waterfront. Stone and sea views, but busy with visitors in the daytime in season.
- **Nature.** The Dim River valley and Sapadere Canyon: green, water and shade. In the canyon the sun disappears early, so shoot in the early afternoon.
- **Antalya.** The old town of Kaleiçi and Konyaaltı seafront, if you are staying closer to Antalya.

More detail on each place, crowds and permissions is in [best photo spots in Alanya](/en/blog/best-photo-spots-alanya/).

## What to wear for a beach photoshoot

1. Plain fabrics without big logos or thin stripes, which flicker in photos.
2. Light, natural colours such as white, beige, soft blue or olive. They sit calmly against sea and sand.
3. Light fabric that moves in the breeze. A dress or a loose shirt brings the frame to life.
4. For couples and families, a shared palette rather than matching outfits.
5. Comfortable shoes to walk to the spot. On the sand, many people go barefoot.
6. If you want a second outfit, tell us in advance so we plan a place to change.

## FAQ

### What is the best time for a photoshoot in Alanya?

The hour before sunset. In summer the shoot starts around 19:00 to 19:15, in winter around 16:40 to 17:00. Early morning is the second good option, with soft light and an empty beach.

### Where can I do a beach photoshoot in Alanya?

Cleopatra Beach is the usual choice because it faces the sunset. The waterfront below the castle and the harbour also work well. For greenery and water, try the Dim River valley.
