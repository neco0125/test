The most reliable way to choose a wedding photographer in Alanya is to see one complete wedding, from getting ready to the last dance, not a highlight reel. Then check three things: whether the style suits you, whether photo and film come from one team, and whether the contract states the hours of coverage and the delivery date.

## Ask to see a whole wedding

Every photographer has ten great frames. The difference shows across a full day: preparations, ceremony, family photos and the evening. Does the quality hold from morning to night?

Light matters most. Many weddings in Alanya and Antalya are outdoors, so look at three moments:

| Time | What to check in the portfolio |
|---|---|
| Midday | Faces without harsh shadows, a white dress that keeps its detail |
| Before sunset | Soft portraits of the couple, natural skin tones |
| Night | Dancing and guests in focus, noise kept under control |

If your venue is booked, ask for examples from similar places: a beach, a garden or a terrace above the sea.

## Style: documentary or posed

Photographers work in different ways: documentary, classic posed, editorial or cinematic. Decide what you like and bring a few examples to the first call.

We film and photograph the day in order and do not force poses. We work with natural light and wait for real moments: the first look, tears at the ceremony, laughter at the table.

## Photo and film from one team

When separate teams shoot photo and video, they compete for the same spots at the key moments. With one team, colour, timing and delivery come from one place.

Our team covers photo, film and, if you want it, drone. The video side includes:

- a wedding film of 2 to 3 hours, recorded continuously from your first entrance to the last moment;
- a 1 to 3 minute clip, edited as a Reel for social media;
- separate audio recording, so vows and speeches are clear;
- a clip cut to music, with colour grading;
- optional aerial shots of the venue, the sea and your guests.

## What the contract should say

Do not book without a written contract. It should state:

1. Start and end time of coverage, or that it is a full day.
2. What you receive: photos, film, clip, aerial footage.
3. The delivery date and what happens if it slips.
4. Cancellation and date change terms.
5. When extra charges can apply: overtime, travel, extra sessions.

Also ask about backup equipment, in case a memory card or camera fails.

## Questions for the first call

- Can we see one full wedding?
- How many people will work on the day?
- Will you visit the venue beforehand?
- Do you record audio separately?
- When will everything be ready, and is that written down?
- Do you deliver vertical versions for social media?

Talk at least once by video call or in person. You will spend the whole day with this person, so you should feel at ease.
