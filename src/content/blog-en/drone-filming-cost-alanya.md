Drone filming in Alanya is not priced from a fixed rate card. Five things shape the cost: how long the shoot lasts, the location and its flight permission, the time of day, how much editing you need and which files are delivered. We quote on duration, location and deliverables.

Below is what moves the price, what to send for a quote and the flight rules that apply in Turkey.

## What affects the cost

| Factor | Why it changes the price |
|---|---|
| Duration | Half a day at a venue and a full day at an event are different workloads |
| Location | Alanya, another part of Antalya or a yacht at sea: travel and prep time change |
| Flight permission | Restricted zones need extra approvals and planning |
| Time of day | Sunset or night fixes the timing and sometimes needs a second day |
| Ground filming | Rooms, a restaurant, people: if the drone alone is not enough, a second camera is needed |
| Editing | Raw footage, a short clip, or a colour-graded promo cut to music |
| Formats | Horizontal 16:9, vertical 9:16, cuts of different lengths |

## Raw footage or a finished film

This is where offers differ most. Some cover only the flight and raw files. Others deliver a ready-to-use film with colour grading, music and editing. If your own editor will cut the material, raw files may be enough. If the video goes straight to Booking, your website or Instagram, you need a finished edit.

We shoot aerial footage in 4K at 50 or 60 fps, which allows smooth slow motion. Delivery is a colour-graded edit cut to music, in horizontal and vertical versions.

## What to send for a quote

The clearer the brief, the more accurate the offer:

- date or date range;
- location: venue name or a map pin;
- subject: hotel, yacht, restaurant, wedding or event;
- where the video will be used: Booking, website, Reels, ads;
- lengths you need, for example one long promo and two short vertical cuts;
- preferred time: daytime, sunset or night;
- whether ground filming is needed.

## Drone rules in Turkey

In Turkey, drones of 500 grams and above, and their pilots, must be registered with the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (SHGM) at [iha.shgm.gov.tr](https://iha.shgm.gov.tr/). Near airports and military sites, flying needs separate permission or is banned. Around Alanya, the area near Gazipaşa-Alanya Airport is one example. Rules change, so check the current requirements with SHGM.

Venues have their own rules too. A hotel, marina or wedding venue may not allow flights, often for guest privacy. Settle this before you fix the date. We check the permission, the size of the area and the light together when planning the flight.

## How to compare offers

- Are editing and colour grading included?
- How many versions do you get, and is there a vertical one?
- What happens if the weather stops the flight?
- Who checks the venue and airspace permission?
- Is the delivery date written down?

The lowest offer often describes a smaller job. Prices only line up when the scope is the same.
