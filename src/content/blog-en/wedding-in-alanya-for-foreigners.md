Foreign couples marry in Turkey in one of two ways: a legal civil marriage at the municipal marriage office, or a symbolic ceremony with the legal registration done at home. A legal marriage needs home-country documents, translated and certified; your consulate and the municipality give the exact list. A symbolic ceremony is simpler on paper but has no legal effect.

We are not lawyers or wedding planners. This is a practical outline; always confirm document requirements with official sources.

## Legal marriage or symbolic ceremony

| | Legal civil marriage | Symbolic ceremony or elopement |
|---|---|---|
| Legal effect | Yes, the marriage is registered under Turkish law | No, it is a celebration without registration |
| Where | Municipal marriage office (evlendirme dairesi) | Any venue: beach, garden, terrace, yacht |
| Paperwork | Documents from home, translation, certification | Agreements with the venue and suppliers |
| Lead time | Longer: documents, deadlines, checks | Depends only on the venue and plan |

Worth knowing: Turkey legally recognises civil marriage. A religious ceremony on its own does not register a marriage.

## Where to ask about documents

Requirements depend on your nationality and can change. The usual order is:

1. **Your country's consulate in Turkey.** It tells you which certificates you need and how to certify them. Typically these include your passport, birth certificate and a certificate of no impediment to marriage. If you were married before, a divorce or death certificate is also needed.
2. **The municipal marriage office.** In Alanya, this sits within Alanya Municipality ([alanya.bel.tr](https://www.alanya.bel.tr/)). It confirms the document list, translation and notarisation requirements, and any medical reports.
3. **A sworn translator and a notary.** Foreign documents are translated into Turkish and certified.

Start early: certificates from home and consular steps take time.

## When to marry in Alanya

The season sets the heat, the light and how busy venues are:

- **June to August.** Hot, with late sunsets around 20:00 to 20:15. Hold the ceremony towards evening.
- **April to May and September to October.** Milder temperatures, comfortable for outdoor weddings.
- **November to March.** Early sunsets, around 17:40 to 18:30. Keep an indoor backup for rain.

## Venue and day plan

Choose the venue type that suits you: beach, garden, villa or a terrace above the sea and the castle. Ask the venue three things: is there a covered area for bad weather, where can you get ready, and are drone flights allowed?

Build the day backwards from sunset. Fix the couple portraits in golden hour first, then the ceremony and preparations. If you are in town for a few days, keep one evening free for a [photoshoot in Alanya](/en/blog/photoshoot-in-alanya/) on the beach.

## FAQ

### Can foreigners legally marry in Turkey?

Yes. The municipal marriage office registers the marriage. The document list depends on nationality, so confirm it with your consulate and the municipality.

### Is a symbolic ceremony a legal marriage?

No. A symbolic ceremony is a celebration without legal effect. You can register the marriage in your home country and hold the ceremony in Turkey for yourselves and your guests.
